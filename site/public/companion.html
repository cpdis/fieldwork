Before Lab 1

The two-minute warm-up

Ask for one thing you assume it cannot do. If you are stuck for an idea:

  • Photograph something handwritten on your desk and ask for it as a table.
  • Paste the last long email you received and ask what it wants from you, and by when.
  • Describe a job you quoted last week and ask what you forgot to price.

Set up a Project that knows your business, so every chat from now on starts from you.

Done when You asked it a question a customer asked you this week, and the answer used your prices and sounded like you.

Instructions to fill in, five lines
Who we are:
Who our customers are:
How we talk:

ChatGPT
  1. Sidebar, New project. Name it after your business.

Lab 4

An admin job, done by an agent

Hand an agent one real multi-step job on a folder of your own files: reconcile two lists, sort and rename a folder of documents, compile quotes from PDFs, or fill a form from a document.

Done when It finished or stopped where you told it to, and you checked one result against the source before approving.

A goal with all five parts
The job: 
Where things are: 
What good means: 
What I want back: 
Where to stop: 
Show me your plan and wait for my okay.
ChatGPT
  1. ChatGPT desktop, Work. Make a copy of the folder first, then pick the copy, or the connected app, it may use.
  2. Write the goal with all five parts.
  3. Read the plan before it starts. Correct it there; that is the cheapest moment.
  4. When it stops, check one item against the source. Then approve, or tell it what to fix.

Lab 5

Turn your task into a skill

Take a job that went well today and save the method as a skill. Then run it on an example it has not seen.

Done when The skill ran on a new example, and you checked the result against what you would have done.

Prompt
Turn what we did into a skill. Name it, say when to use it, list the steps and the checks.
ChatGPT
  1. In the chat where the job went well, paste the prompt.
  2. Say: Save this as a skill. Confirm when it asks.
  3. New chat, new input. Does it use the skill without being told?
  4. Something wrong? Fix the skill, not the output. Run it again.
Claude
  1. In the chat where the job went well, paste the prompt.
  2. Ask Claude to package it as a skill, then save it from the chat, or upload it in Settings, Capabilities, Skills.
  3. New chat, new input. Does it use the skill without being told?
  4. Something wrong? Fix the skill, not the output. Run it again.
Take-home lab

Build your own glue (take-home, about 30 minutes)

Pick the one place you copy and paste between two tools. Have the agent build the view or the connection, working on exports, not on your live systems.

Done when It ran on your sample, and three rows you checked by hand match the source.

Prompt 1
Every week I copy [what] from [tool A] into [tool B]. Exports of both are here. Build me [a one-page view of ... OR a file I can import into ...]. Ask me three questions first.
Prompt 2
Write a short instruction note (a README): what this reads, what it makes, and how I run it next week.
ChatGPT
  1. Make a folder called glue. Put in a small export from each tool: 10 to 20 rows, no bank or card details.
  2. Open the folder in Codex and paste the first prompt, with your own words in the brackets.
  3. Open what it made. Check three rows by hand against the source.
  4. Paste the second prompt.
Claude
  1. Make a folder called glue. Put in a small export from each tool: 10 to 20 rows, no bank or card details.
  2. Open the folder in the Code tab and paste the first prompt, with your own words in the brackets.
  3. Open what it made. Check three rows by hand against the source.

Lab 7

One job that runs without you

Pick one thing you check every week and set it up as a scheduled task, with the rules written into it. Nothing connected? Pick a job that needs no connector: a weekly check of a supplier's public price page, or a summary of one folder.

Done when The task exists, with its rules written in, and it ran once when you pressed run.

Prompt
Every [day] at [time], [the job]. You may read and draft. Ask before you send anything. Never pay or delete. Tell me what you could not find.
ChatGPT
  1. Pick the job: a Monday brief, an inbox digest, a price check on a public web page, a to-do refresh.
  2. In a new chat, paste the prompt with your own words in the brackets.
  3. Find it under Scheduled in the sidebar. Run it once now.
  4. Read the result. Tighten the wording, then leave it on.
Claude
  1. Pick the job: a Monday brief, an inbox digest, a price check on a public web page, a to-do refresh.
  2. Scheduled, New task, Create with Claude. Paste the prompt with your own words in the brackets.
  3. Check the approval mode, then run it once now.
  4. Read the result. Tighten the wording, then leave it on.
Homework

Homework: a one-page AI use policy

Ten minutes, in either app. Run it in your business Project, so it already knows what you do. Privacy law is good practice for everyone and a legal duty for some businesses. This is not legal advice.

Prompt
Interview me, one question at a time, to write a one-page AI use policy for my business. Cover five things: what we use AI for, what never goes in, who checks work before it leaves, what we tell clients, and what we do if it stops working. Use plain words. Keep it to one page.
